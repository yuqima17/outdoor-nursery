# Outdoor Nursery Privacy Policy Draft

Effective date: Draft for private beta

Outdoor Nursery is a small app for helping parents and caregivers find baby- and kid-friendly places to go, such as parks, playgrounds, and outdoor malls.

This draft explains what the MVP collects today. It should be reviewed before TestFlight, a wider beta, or any public App Store release.

## What We Collect

The current MVP may store:

- Saved place IDs on your device.
- Quick feedback selections on your device.
- An anonymous device ID on your device.
- Anonymous quick feedback rows in our backend when feedback is submitted.

Quick feedback may include:

- Place ID.
- Anonymous device ID.
- Feedback type, such as `easy_parking` or `info_changed`.
- Timestamp.
- Basic metadata needed to understand which button was tapped.

## What We Do Not Collect

The current MVP does not ask for or collect:

- Parent or caregiver name.
- Child name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Photos.
- Contacts.
- Payment information.
- Live GPS location.
- Precise location history.
- Child birthday, school, daycare, medical information, or other child-specific personal details.

## Saved Places

Saved places are stored locally on your phone in this MVP.

They are not synced across devices. If you delete the app, local saved places may be removed by your device.

## Anonymous Quick Feedback

Quick feedback is used to understand whether place information may need review.

For example, if several people report that parking was hard or information changed, we may manually review that place.

Quick feedback is not:

- Publicly displayed as a review.
- Used to create public user profiles.
- Used for advertising.
- Used to automatically change public place facts.

Parent feedback is reviewed before it affects public place information.

## Anonymous Device ID

The app stores an anonymous device ID to reduce duplicate feedback and understand whether multiple devices reported the same issue.

The anonymous device ID is not a user account.

We do not use it for advertising or tracking across other apps.

## Location

Outdoor Nursery stores coordinates for places and can open external directions links.

The current MVP does not collect your live location or background location.

If location-based features are added later, they should be reviewed separately before release.

## Children’s Privacy

Outdoor Nursery is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children to submit personal information.

The MVP does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Please do not submit child names, photos, school/daycare information, medical details, or other child-specific personal information through feedback or support channels.

## Backend

Outdoor Nursery currently uses Supabase to store published place data and anonymous quick feedback.

Public app users should not be able to read raw feedback data. Feedback is private operational data used for manual review.

## Data Retention

Retention rules are not final yet.

Before wider beta or public release, decide:

- How long anonymous feedback should be retained.
- Whether users can request feedback deletion.
- Whether anonymous device IDs should expire or rotate.
