# App Store Listing Copy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This document is the copy/paste source for the first public App Store listing for Outdoor Nursery.

Release position:

- iOS only.
- iPhone only.
- United States only.
- First public data set is intentionally small, but public listing copy should not lock the product to one city or metro area.
- Free.
- No account required.
- No ads.
- No live location tracking.
- Place details can change, so the listing should avoid promising official or real-time accuracy.

Apple metadata limits to keep in mind:

- App name: 30 characters max.
- Subtitle: 30 characters max.
- Promotional text: 170 characters max.
- Description: 4000 characters max.
- Keywords: 100 characters total.

## Recommended Copy

Name:

```text
Outdoor Nursery
```

Subtitle:

```text
Baby-friendly outings
```

Promotional text:

```text
Find low-stress outings with practical parent notes for parks, playgrounds, and outdoor malls.
```

Keywords:

```text
baby,toddler,kids,family,park,playground,stroller,outings,parents,restroom
```

Description:

```text
Outdoor Nursery helps parents and caregivers find practical places to go with babies and young kids.

Browse parks, playgrounds, and outdoor malls with quick notes for the details that matter before leaving home:

- Best-fit age guidance
- Stroller friendliness
- Restrooms and baby-care notes
- Parking and parking fee guidance
- Admission and reservation notes
- Weather fit, shade, food nearby, and helpful parent notes

Save places on your phone, open directions, and use quick anonymous feedback to tell us whether things like parking, stroller access, crowd level, cleanliness, or place information matched your visit.

Outdoor Nursery is built for planning, not emergency or official travel guidance. Place details can change. Please confirm hours, fees, closures, reservations, and facilities with the official place source before a real trip.

The first release starts with a small, curated set of family outing ideas and will grow over time.
```

What is new in this version:

```text
First public release.
```

Review notes:

```text
No login is required. Please browse Home, search or filter places, open a place detail page, save and unsave a place, open directions, and try the anonymous quick feedback voting controls.

The app does not collect child personal information, contacts, photos, payment information, or live GPS location. Place details can change and should be confirmed with official sources before real trips, especially hours, fees, reservations, closures, and facilities.
```
