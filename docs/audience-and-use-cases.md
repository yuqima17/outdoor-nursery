# Audience And Use Cases

## Primary Audience

- Parents with babies or toddlers.
- Grandparents, relatives, nannies, and other caregivers.
- Families visiting a new city or neighborhood.
- Caregivers looking for low-stress outdoor activities.

## Common Use Cases

### Quick Nearby Search

A caregiver wants to leave the house soon and needs a nearby place that is easy with a stroller, has shade, and has bathrooms.

### Weekend Outing

A family wants to compare several parks, trails, or outdoor malls and choose one that fits nap timing, food needs, parking, and walking distance.

### Travel Planning

A caregiver visiting another city wants toddler-friendly outdoor places near a hotel, relative's house, or vacation rental.

### Weather-Aware Discovery

A family wants to find a place that works for the current weather, such as shaded parks on hot days or paved paths after rain.

### Caregiver Contribution

A user visits a place and adds notes about stroller access, bathroom quality, construction, crowding, playground condition, or hidden helpful details.

## Key User Questions

- Can I bring a stroller?
- Is this safe for a toddler who wants to walk?
- How long is the easiest route?
- Are bathrooms close and usable?
- Is there shade or indoor backup nearby?
- Is parking simple?
- Is this good for a baby, a toddler, or both?
- What should I bring?
