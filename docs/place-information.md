# Place Information

Outdoor Nursery should describe places using caregiver-focused information rather than only standard location metadata.

## Place Types

- Parks
- Trails
- Playgrounds
- Botanical gardens
- Outdoor malls
- Walkable plazas
- Waterfront paths
- Beaches
- Farms and orchards
- Nature centers
- Picnic areas

## Baby And Toddler Friendly Signals

- Stroller-friendly paths
- Smooth or paved surfaces
- Short route options
- Low traffic exposure
- Fenced or contained play areas
- Shade coverage
- Seating
- Bathrooms
- Baby care facilities
- Changing tables
- Family restrooms
- Nursing-friendly spots
- Nursing-friendly spots
- Food, snacks, or cafes nearby
- Drinking water
- Parking proximity
- Public transit access
- Quiet areas
- Indoor or sheltered backup nearby

## Possible Rating Dimensions

- Stroller access
- Toddler safety
- Bathroom convenience
- Shade and weather comfort
- Ease of parking
- Cleanliness
- Crowding
- Route difficulty
- Snack and meal convenience
- Overall caregiver confidence

## Useful Place Notes

Each place should include short, practical notes such as:

- Best entrance for strollers.
- Where to park.
- Whether bathrooms are open seasonally.
- Which path is easiest.
- Where shade is strongest.
- Whether the playground is better for babies, toddlers, or older kids.
- Any hazards, such as steep drops, water access, bikes, mud, stairs, or heavy crowds.
