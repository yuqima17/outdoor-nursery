# Product Purpose

## Vision

Outdoor Nursery helps caregivers confidently choose outdoor places that fit the needs of babies and toddlers.

The product should feel calm, trustworthy, and useful in the moment before an outing. It should reduce uncertainty by turning scattered place information into caregiver-centered guidance.

## Problem

Parents and caregivers often want to spend time outside with babies and toddlers, but it can be hard to know whether a place is actually practical.

Common questions include:

- Is the trail stroller-friendly?
- Are there clean bathrooms or baby care facilities?
- Is there shade?
- Is parking close to the entrance?
- Is the route short enough for a toddler?
- Is there a place to sit, feed, or calm a child?
- Is the area too crowded, steep, muddy, noisy, or exposed?

General place listings rarely answer these questions clearly.

## Solution

Outdoor Nursery organizes outdoor destinations around the needs of young children and their caregivers.

Instead of only listing places, the app should provide a family-readiness profile for each location. This profile can combine map data, official place details, caregiver reviews, photos, tags, and safety notes.

## Product Principles

- Practical before promotional.
- Clear enough to decide quickly.
- Honest about tradeoffs.
- Designed for one-handed mobile use.
- Respectful of caregiver time, stress, and attention.
- Focused on real-world details, not generic ratings.
