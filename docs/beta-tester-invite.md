# Beta Tester Invite

Use these messages to invite a friend to test Outdoor Nursery.

## Short Message

Hi! I am building a small app called Outdoor Nursery. It helps parents find baby- and kid-friendly places to go, like parks, playgrounds, and outdoor malls.

Could you try it for 5 minutes and tell me:

- What you think the app is for
- Whether the place cards make sense
- Whether the detail page answers what you would want to know before going
- Anything confusing, missing, or annoying

The data is still prototype data, so please do not treat it as fully verified trip advice yet.

## Slightly Longer Message

Hi! I am working on an early mobile app called Outdoor Nursery. The idea is to help parents quickly find low-stress places to go with babies and young kids, especially when details like parking, restrooms, stroller access, food nearby, shade, and age fit matter.

Would you be willing to test it for 5-10 minutes?

Please try:

1. Open the Home screen and tell me what you think the app does.
2. Search or filter for a place you might actually consider.
3. Open a place detail page.
4. Save and unsave a place.
5. Tap a few quick feedback buttons.
6. Tell me what feels useful, confusing, or missing.

No need to check whether the place data is perfectly accurate yet. I am mainly testing whether the app idea and screens make sense.

## Questions To Send After They Try It

- What did you think the app was for?
- Which place would you open first?
- Did any card or detail feel confusing?
- Did the app answer the things you care about before leaving home?
- Would you use something like this for a casual outing?
- What would make it more useful?

## Boundaries To Mention

- This is an MVP prototype.
- Place details may need official confirmation before a real trip.
- No account is required.
- No child information is collected.
- Quick feedback is anonymous in this version.
