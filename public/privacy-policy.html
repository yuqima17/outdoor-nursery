Outdoor Nursery Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Outdoor Nursery is a small app for helping parents and caregivers find baby- and kid-friendly places to go, such as parks, playgrounds, and outdoor malls.

This policy explains what the app collects today.

What We Collect

The current app may store:

  • Saved place IDs on your device.
  • Quick feedback selections on your device.
  • An anonymous device ID on your device.
  • Anonymous quick feedback rows in our backend when feedback is submitted.

Quick feedback may include place ID, anonymous device ID, feedback type, timestamp, and basic metadata needed to understand which button was tapped.

What We Do Not Collect

The current app does not ask for or collect parent names, child names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts, payment information, live GPS location, precise location history, child birthdays, school/daycare information, medical information, or other child-specific personal details.

Saved Places

Saved places are stored locally on your phone. They are not synced across devices. If you delete the app, local saved places may be removed by your device.

Anonymous Quick Feedback

Quick feedback is used to understand whether place information may need review. For example, if several people report that parking was hard or information changed, we may manually review that place.

Quick feedback is not publicly displayed as a review, used to create public user profiles, used for advertising, or used to automatically change public place facts. Parent feedback is reviewed before it affects public place information.

Anonymous Device ID

The app stores an anonymous device ID to reduce duplicate feedback and understand whether multiple devices reported the same issue. The anonymous device ID is not a user account. We do not use it for advertising or tracking across other apps.

Location

Outdoor Nursery stores coordinates for places and can open external directions links. The current app does not collect your live location or background location.

Children's Privacy

Outdoor Nursery is designed for parents and caregivers, not for children to submit personal information. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Please do not submit child names, photos, school/daycare information, medical details, or other child-specific personal information through feedback or support channels.

Backend

Outdoor Nursery currently uses Supabase to store published place data and anonymous quick feedback. Public app users should not be able to read raw feedback data. Feedback is private operational data used for manual review.

Data Retention

Anonymous feedback may be retained while it is useful for reviewing place information and improving app quality.

Changes
