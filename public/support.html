Outdoor Nursery Support

Last updated: August 10, 2026

Outdoor Nursery helps parents and caregivers find baby- and kid-friendly places to go in the Bay Area, including parks, playgrounds, and outdoor malls.

For support, questions, or place information updates, email outdoornursery@gmail.com.

What To Include

If you are reporting a problem, it helps to include:

  • The place name.
  • What changed or what looked wrong.
  • Your device model and iOS version if the app itself is not working.
  • A short description of what you expected to happen.

Place Information

Place details can change. Please confirm hours, fees, closures, and facilities with the official place source before a real trip. Parent feedback helps us decide which places need manual review, but feedback does not automatically change public place facts.

Privacy

Please do not send child names, child photos, school or daycare details, medical information, or other child-specific personal information in support messages.

Read the Outdoor Nursery Privacy Policy for more information about current app data handling.
